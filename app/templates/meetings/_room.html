Only audio is recorded, and only while you both agree. If either of you withdraws, or the meeting ends, recording stops. The call can carry on without it. If you both agree, the recorded audio is sent to Groq (a third-party AI service) to be transcribed into text, and the text is used to create private draft notes for the FC to review.
